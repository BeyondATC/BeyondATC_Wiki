# Radios & COM2

## COM2: Your Second Radio

!!! warning "COM2 is Monitor only"
    COM2 is **monitor only**: you can listen to any frequency, but you can only speak to BeyondATC on COM1.

COM2 gives you the flexibility to listen to other frequencies in BeyondATC, allowing up to two simultaneous transmissions. With the **Cockpit Radio Controls** option enabled, COM2 follows your cockpit audio panel and its own volume knob.

!!! note "Quick Tuning"
    **Right-click frequencies within the BeyondATC frequencies menu** to tune it on COM2.

- The radio strip shows what you're hearing: **COM2: Boston Center [Monitor Only]**.
- COM2 traffic appears in the message log with a **[COM2]** prefix.
