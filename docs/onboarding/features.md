# Features

!!! question "Where can I suggest a feature?"
    If you want to share your ideas, please send your suggestion here: [https://beyondatc.nolt.io](https://beyondatc.nolt.io)

 
 
BeyondATC provides the following:

- Worldwide IFR with realistic procedures and phraseology for FAA and ICAO
- A world-class voice recognition system, designed for simmers all over the world
- A fully local voice model with worldwide accents
- A premium cloud voice model that you can choose to use with premium characters  
- An elegant and simple UI 
- Full SimBrief integration
- The ability to interact with ATC via Voice, Buttons or AI Co-Pilot
- Dynamic Runway/SID/STAR selection based on our own extensive collection of airport ops
- PIREPS, Direct-Tos, Altitude Changes and more
- ILS Approaches, Visual Approaches based on weather, vectoring and more
- An in-sim toolbar to control BeyondATC (especially useful for VR and sim pits)
- Custom Traffic injection and control based on up to worldwide traffic schedules
- Live traffic injection for those with a Navigraph Unlimited subscription 
- Support for multi-leg flights with turnarounds
- CPDLC (with compatible aircraft)
- GSX Integration
- Independent COM1 and COM2 radio support (COM2 is monitor only) 
- Supports MSFS 2020 and MSFS 2024

On our experimental branch, we are currently working on:

- Full VFR flight rules, focusing on procedures from the US, UK and AUS

List of features that are NOT available but are planned:

- Emergency Procedures
- Oceanic Procedures
- Holds
- IFR/VFR Transition
